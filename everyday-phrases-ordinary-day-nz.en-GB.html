Everyday phrases for an ordinary day: say it, then hear the reply

If you search for everyday phrases in New Zealand, you probably want the sentences you would actually use on a normal day overseas: ordering at a café, asking in a shop, checking in at work. Knowing your own line is half of it. The other half is understanding what comes back.

Walk through an ordinary day

DailyMate organises 8,400 complete phrases into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, from greetings, food and shopping to work, health and travel. A simple way to use them is to follow your own day:

  1. Morning: greetings for the people you meet first.
  2. Midday: food and shopping, where you need to ask and understand an answer quickly.
  3. Afternoon: work, if you will use the language on the job.
  4. Just in case: health and travel, so you are not looking for words when you need them.

Each phrase comes with its reply

DailyMate teaches complete sentences and the natural replies that follow, so you learn what people really say rather than isolated vocabulary. Practising both sides of a short exchange helps when the other person answers back.

Beginner, intermediate, advanced

Every topic has 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases. Start with the essentials, add richer intermediate wording, then move into more precise expressions in the topics you use most.

Listen, save, come back

Hear complete phrases with the system voice suited to the chosen language. Save the ones you will want again, and pick up from the progress kept separately for each of the 47 learning languages.

One phrase on your Home Screen

The interactive Home Screen widget shows a phrase and lets you move to the next one or save it. Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, saved items and progress on your wrist.

What it costs

DailyMate is a paid app: one purchase covers the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive widget and Apple Watch, with no subscription.
