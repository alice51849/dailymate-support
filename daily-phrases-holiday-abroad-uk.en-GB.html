Daily phrases for a holiday abroad: start two weeks before you fly

Most people look up daily phrases a few days before a trip and then freeze when the other person answers. Two weeks of one situation a day, learned together with the reply, is usually a practical way to prepare for the first exchanges of a holiday. Start with the Travel topic at beginner level.

Two weeks, one situation a day

DaysSituationsGoal
1–4Greetings, café, restaurant, shoppingSay the phrase and understand the most likely reply.
5–8Travel and getting aroundAsk one clear question and follow the answer.
9–12Health and anything you meet oftenHave one phrase ready for the situations you worry about.
13–14ReviewOpen your favourites and say each phrase and reply once without reading.

Why the reply matters as much as the phrase

A phrase on its own only gets you halfway; the conversation continues with whatever the other person says back. In DailyMate each of the 8,400 practical phrases comes with the natural reply that follows, organised into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations in 47 learning languages.

Which level to use before a trip

Stay at beginner level for the trip itself: every topic has 200 beginner phrases, plus 200 intermediate and 200 advanced ones for after you are home. Favourites and progress are kept separately for each language, so a holiday in Spain and one in Japan do not mix.

Reviewing while you pack

The interactive Home Screen widget shows a phrase, moves to the next one or saves it, and Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, favourites and progress on your wrist, handy in a queue at the airport. For a week-by-week routine at home, see the seven-day daily phrases plan.
