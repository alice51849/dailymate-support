Daily phrases: a seven-day plan you can actually keep

Most daily phrases habits stop after a week because each day starts from nothing. This plan gives every day one situation, one complete phrase and the reply you are likely to hear, so the week adds up to conversations you can use.

The seven days

DaySituationWhat to do
MondayGreetingsLearn one greeting and its natural reply; say both aloud.
TuesdayCafé or restaurantLearn how to order and what the server usually says back.
WednesdayShoppingLearn one question about price or size and the reply.
ThursdayGetting aroundLearn how to ask for a station or stop and the directions you hear.
FridayWorkLearn one phrase you would use at work and the reply.
SaturdayTravelLearn one hotel or airport phrase and the reply that follows.
SundayReviewOpen your favourites and say each phrase and its reply once without reading.

Why one situation a day works

A phrase tied to a real situation is easier to recall when that situation happens. DailyMate organises 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, and each phrase comes with the natural reply that follows, so every day of the plan has material ready.

When to move up a level

Every topic has 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases. Repeat the week at beginner level until Sunday's review comes out without reading, then run the same seven situations at intermediate level.

Keeping it going on busy days

The interactive Home Screen widget lets you view a phrase, move to the next one or save it without opening the app, and Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, favourites and progress on your wrist. A busy day can still be a phrase day.
