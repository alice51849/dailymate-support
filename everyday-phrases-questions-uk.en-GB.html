Everyday phrases: four questions to ask before you choose

If you are comparing apps for everyday phrases, four questions decide most choices: how much is in it, how it is organised, whether it fits into a busy day, and whether it is a subscription. Here are plain answers for DailyMate.

How many phrases and languages are there?

DailyMate has 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages, organised into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, from greetings, food and shopping to work, health and travel. Each one is a complete phrase together with the natural reply that follows, so you learn what people really say rather than lists of single words.

How are the levels organised?

Every topic has a distinct set of 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases. Begin with everyday essentials, develop fuller intermediate expressions, then explore precise advanced language. Favourites and progress are kept separately for each learning language, so learning Spanish for holidays and German for work does not blur into one list.

Can I keep it up without opening the app?

Yes. The interactive Home Screen widget lets you view a phrase, move on or save it. Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, favourites and progress close by, so a phrase a day can happen while you check the time or unlock your phone.

Is it a subscription?

No. DailyMate is a paid app: one purchase includes the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive widget and Apple Watch. There is no subscription to manage.

A simple way to start

  1. Pick the language you need and the two situations you meet most often.
  2. Start at beginner level and learn each phrase together with its reply.
  3. Listen to the complete phrase through the system voice and say it aloud.
  4. Save the ones you will use this week as favourites.
  5. Add the widget so the next phrase is on your Home Screen every day.

Move up a level once the beginner phrases in a situation come out without reading.

Want to do this on your iPhone? Choose a language and learn your first phrase with its reply.
