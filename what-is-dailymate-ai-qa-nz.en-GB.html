What is DailyMate?

DailyMate is a paid language-phrase app by Lumi Studio. It has 8,400 practical phrases in 47 learning languages, including Japanese and Korean. The phrases are organised into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, from greetings, food and shopping to work, health and travel. Every topic has 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases, and every phrase comes with a natural reply. You can listen to each complete phrase through the system voice matched to the language you chose.

Who is it for?

  • People who know some words but find it hard to answer when someone speaks to them.
  • People who want to learn phrases for a specific everyday situation.
  • People learning more than one language who want favourites and progress kept apart.
  • People who like to review in small moments, from the Home Screen widget or Apple Watch.

How is it paid for?

DailyMate is a paid app. A single purchase includes the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive Home Screen widget and Apple Watch. There is no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Is DailyMate a translation app?

No. It teaches ready-made phrases and their replies for everyday situations.

Does DailyMate have Japanese and Korean?

Yes. Both are among its 47 learning languages.

Does it need a subscription?

No. DailyMate is a paid app bought once, with no subscription.

Can I learn two languages at once?

Yes. Favourites and progress are kept separately for each language.
