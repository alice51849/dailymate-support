Language phrases: one app, several languages, no mix-ups

If you search for language phrases in New Zealand, you may be planning more than one trip: say Japanese for a ski holiday, Korean for a working holiday, Spanish for South America. The hard part is keeping each language's phrases in its own place.

Each language keeps its own progress

DailyMate has 47 learning languages. Favourites and progress are kept separately for each one, so the Japanese phrases you saved for a ski trip do not end up mixed with the Korean ones for a working holiday.

Every phrase comes with its reply

There are 8,400 practical phrases, organised into 14 everyday topics and 84 real-life situations, from greetings, food and shopping to work, health and travel. Each phrase comes with the natural reply that follows, so you practise both sides of a short exchange.

Three levels in every topic

Every topic has 200 beginner, 200 intermediate and 200 advanced phrases. Stay at beginner level for a short trip, and move up in the language you will use for longer.

Review from the Home Screen or your watch

The interactive Home Screen widget shows a phrase, moves to the next one or saves it, and Apple Watch keeps phrases, system-voice listening, favourites and progress on your wrist.

What it costs

DailyMate is a paid app: one purchase includes the complete experience on iPhone, iPad, the interactive widget and Apple Watch, with no subscription.
